GEAR: Immersive 3D, AR and VR Learning

Upload a 3D model, place interactive hotspots on it and let students explore, answer quizzes and learn together, right inside Moodle.

Features

  • 3D viewer: glTF / GLB models (also Draco and Meshopt compressed, several per scene) with orbit, pan and zoom controls, adjustable lighting and background.
  • Hotspots: information, quiz, audio, video and teleport points placed directly on the model; the authoring gizmo moves them along the X, Y and Z axes.
  • Spatial quizzes: multiple-choice questions in the scene. Answers are checked on the server and the points go to the Moodle gradebook.
  • Escape-room style unlocking: a hotspot can stay hidden until the student solves a given quiz.
  • Teacher analytics, a group-aware leaderboard and text-to-speech narration.
  • Completion rules: open every hotspot and/or earn a minimum of quiz points.
  • WebXR: VR (and AR where the device supports it) without installing an app. Works with the Moodle Mobile app.
  • Optional AI assistant for hotspot texts and quiz questions, using the AI providers of Moodle (off by default).
  • Optional collaborative mode: shared sessions with avatars, voice and text chat (off by default for new installs).
  • Accessible: keyboard navigation, screen reader announcements, labelled dialogs and reduced-motion support.
  • Moodle integration: gradebook, backup and restore, Privacy API, course reset, events, completion and group mode.

What's new in 1.7.1

  • Security: quiz scores can no longer be forged, correct answers and locked hotspots are no longer sent to students, hotspot content and report output are sanitised, model files are served only to people who can view the activity, and WebRTC peers must be participants of the session.
  • AI assistant on the Moodle AI subsystem: providers, models, quotas and the usage policy are managed in Moodle's AI settings; the plugin no longer stores an API key.
  • Collaborative mode is now an admin setting with an optional own PeerJS server; random peer ids are used.
  • Grades: every quiz submission is counted, the best score per hotspot is kept and the total is scaled to the activity grade.
  • Accessibility and performance: real dialogs with focus handling, labelled controls, rendering and polling pause in hidden tabs, PeerJS loads only when needed.
  • New: completion rules (open every hotspot, minimum quiz points), group mode, Draco and Meshopt compressed models, several models per scene with loading progress, and AR Quick Look on iPhone and iPad through an optional USDZ file.
  • Fixed: the AR and VR buttons stay enabled in WebXR browsers (they were always disabled before).
  • Quality: PHPUnit and Behat tests, events, a scheduled cleanup task, course reset, and CI on Moodle 4.5 and 5.0 - 5.3.

Requirements

  • Moodle 4.5 LTS to 5.3, PHP 8.2 or later.
  • A browser with WebGL; VR and AR need a WebXR-capable browser and device. On iPhone and iPad the 3D viewer works; AR there uses AR Quick Look with an optional USDZ file.

Privacy

Models, hotspots, grades and analytics stay on your site. Only two optional features contact outside services: the AI assistant sends the text a teacher types to the AI provider the administrator set up in Moodle's AI settings, and collaborative mode connects participants through a PeerJS broker (the public PeerJS cloud or your own server) while chat and audio travel directly between their browsers. The plugin implements the Moodle Privacy API.
