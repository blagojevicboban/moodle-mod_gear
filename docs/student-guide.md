# GEAR – student guide

A GEAR activity shows a 3D model that you can explore. Coloured points (**hotspots**) on the model give you information, questions, sounds or videos.

## Move around

| Action | Mouse | Touch screen | Keyboard |
|--------|-------|--------------|----------|
| Rotate | Left button + drag | One finger | – |
| Pan | Right button + drag | Two fingers | – |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel | Pinch | – |
| Next / previous hotspot | – | – | Tab to the scene, then arrow keys |
| Open the hotspot | Click | Tap (long-press for a preview) | Enter or Space |
| Close the popup | Close button | Close button | Escape |

The buttons in the corner of the scene open the hotspot list, the leaderboard, voice chat, text chat and this help.

## Hotspots

- **Information:** read the text, or use the speaker button to hear it.
- **Quiz:** choose an answer and press submit. A correct answer gives points that count towards your grade. A quiz can unlock more hotspots.
- **Audio / video:** play the sound or video. Audio gets louder when you get closer to it.
- **Teleport:** takes you to another place in the scene.

## Working together

If classmates open the same activity at the same time, you see them as small spheres. You can write in the chat and, if you allow the
microphone in your browser, talk to them. Your browser connects directly to theirs; nothing you say is recorded in Moodle.

## 3D Tools

- **Snapshot:** Click the camera icon in the toolbar to save a screenshot of the 3D scene to your device.
- **Cross-section:** Click the cube/cut icon to see inside the model. Pick an axis (X, Y, or Z) and drag the slider to adjust the cutting depth.
- **Measure distance:** Click the ruler icon, then click two points on the 3D model to see the distance between them. Click Clear or press Escape to finish.

## Tips

- If the scene stays black, check that your browser supports WebGL and that hardware acceleration is on.
- For VR or AR use a device and browser with WebXR support. On iPhone and iPad the 3D scene works, but AR is not available.
- Use headphones with voice chat to avoid echo.
