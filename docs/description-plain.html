GEAR: Immersive 3D, AR and VR Learning

Upload a 3D model, place interactive hotspots on it and let students explore, answer quizzes and learn together, right inside Moodle.

What's New in v1.9.1

  • Model upload fix (v1.9.1): fixed .glb, .gltf, .bin and .usdz uploads on standard Moodle sites, plus size and MIME checks for hotspot media.
  • 3D Scene Tools: PNG snapshot export, interactive X/Y/Z cross-section clipping planes, and a two-point 3D surface measurement tool.
  • Direct Hotspot Media Upload: upload audio and video files directly in the hotspot form into Moodle's own file storage without external hosting.
  • Scene JSON Export & Import: export your scene (camera, clipping plane, hotspots, quizzes, prerequisites) to .gear.json and import it into another activity.

Overview

GEAR (Geospatial Educational Augmented Reality) is a Moodle activity module that brings interactive 3D, AR and VR learning into Moodle. Educators upload 3D models, annotate them with hotspots and assessment points, and students explore, collaborate and are graded inside Moodle.

Core features

  • 3D viewer and interaction: glTF / GLB models (Draco, Meshopt and KTX2 compressed, several per scene) with orbit, pan, zoom, adjustable lighting, glTF animations and material variants.
  • Interactive hotspots: Information, quiz, audio, video (MP4, YouTube, Vimeo) and teleport points placed directly on the model with visual X/Y/Z gizmos.
  • Spatial quizzes and completion: single choice, multiple choice or typed answers (with wildcards), written by hand or copied from the course question bank. Checked on the server and synced with the Moodle gradebook, with custom completion rules.
  • Guided tours and branching: step-by-step camera tours (open hotspots, play animations, switch materials), escape-room style quiz unlocking and teleport waypoints.
  • Optional AI assistant and text-to-speech: generate hotspot texts and quiz questions via Moodle's AI subsystem (off by default); information hotspots can be read aloud.
  • Optional collaborative mode: shared sessions with 3D avatars and peer-to-peer voice and text chat, group aware (off by default).
  • Student progress, analytics and leaderboard: per-student progress report with CSV/Excel export, Report Builder source, hotspot statistics, leaderboard and positional audio.
  • Accessible and fast: keyboard navigation, screen reader announcements, scene text alternative, reduced motion support (checked with axe), and adaptive render resolution for heavy models and mobile devices.

AR/VR and mobile

  • WebXR ready: one-click VR, and AR where the device supports it, without installing an app.
  • iPhone and iPad: the 3D viewer works; upload a .usdz file with the same name as the model for AR Quick Look.
  • Moodle app: shows description and student progress, and opens the 3D scene in the browser with automatic login.

Requirements

  • Moodle: 4.5 LTS to 5.3 (for Moodle 4.1 to 4.4 use version 1.7.0)
  • PHP: 8.2 or later
  • Browser: modern browser with WebGL; VR and AR need a WebXR-capable browser and device
  • AI assistant (optional): an AI provider with text generation configured in Moodle's AI settings

Privacy

Models, hotspots, grades and analytics stay on your site and are covered by the Moodle Privacy API. Only two optional features contact outside services: the AI assistant sends teacher prompts to the configured Moodle AI provider, and collaborative mode connects participants via a PeerJS broker while chat and audio travel peer-to-peer.

How it works

  1. Upload: add your .glb or .gltf models (optionally a .usdz for iPhone AR).
  2. Annotate: Shift + click on the model to place hotspots and assessment points.
  3. Engage: students explore, collaborate and complete the integrated activities.
