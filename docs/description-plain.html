GEAR: Immersive 3D, AR and VR Learning

Upload a 3D model, place interactive hotspots on it and let students explore, answer quizzes and learn together, right inside Moodle.

What's New in v1.8.0

  • Guided tours, animations and material variants: walk students through the scene step by step (each step can open a hotspot, play an animation or switch the material), play the animations of a glTF model and switch its material variants.
  • Richer quizzes: one correct answer, several correct answers or a typed answer, and quiz questions copied from the course question bank.
  • Student progress: per student hotspots opened, quizzes solved, points and grade in the activity report, with details and download, plus a Report Builder source for custom reports.
  • Large models and phones: KTX2 compressed textures, lower render resolution for heavy models and touch screens, and a warning for teachers when a model is too heavy.
  • Accessibility and the Moodle app: Moodle dialogs, a text alternative for the 3D scene and checked with axe; the app shows the student's progress and opens the scene in the browser.

Overview

GEAR (Geospatial Educational Augmented Reality) is a Moodle activity module that bridges the gap between traditional e-learning and immersive 3D technology. Educators upload 3D models, annotate them with hotspots and assessment points, and students explore, collaborate and are graded inside Moodle.

Core features

3D viewer and interaction

glTF / GLB models (also Draco, Meshopt and KTX2 compressed, several per scene) with orbit, pan and zoom, adjustable lighting and background, glTF animations (clip, speed, autoplay) and material variants.

Interactive hotspots

Information, quiz, audio, video (MP4, YouTube, Vimeo) and teleport points placed directly on the model, with visual X/Y/Z gizmos for precise placement.

Spatial quizzes and completion

Questions in the scene with one correct answer, several correct answers or a typed answer (with wildcards), written by hand or copied from the course question bank. Checked on the server and synced with the Moodle gradebook. Completion rules: open every hotspot and/or earn a minimum of quiz points.

Guided tours and escape-room style unlocking

Step by step tours: the camera flies to each view, and a step can open a hotspot, play an animation or switch the material. Branching scenarios: a hotspot stays hidden until the student solves the required quiz. Teleport waypoints guide the camera.

Optional AI assistant and text-to-speech

Generate hotspot texts and quiz questions with the AI providers of Moodle (off by default). Information hotspots can be read aloud.

Optional collaborative mode

Shared sessions with 3D avatars and peer-to-peer voice and text chat, group aware. Off by default for new installs.

Student progress, analytics and leaderboard

Progress of every student (hotspots opened, quizzes solved, points, grade, last activity) with a details page and download as CSV or Excel, a Report Builder source for custom reports, hotspot statistics, a leaderboard, and positional audio that reacts to the student's distance.

Accessible and fast

Keyboard navigation, screen reader announcements, a text alternative for the scene, labelled dialogs and reduced motion support (checked with axe); lower render resolution for heavy models, phones and VR headsets.

AR/VR and mobile

  • WebXR ready: one-click VR, and AR where the device supports it, without installing an app.
  • iPhone and iPad: the 3D viewer works; for AR upload a .usdz file with the same name as the model and it opens in AR Quick Look.
  • Moodle app: shows the description and the student's progress, and opens the 3D scene in the browser with automatic login (the 3D viewer itself needs a browser).

Requirements

  • Moodle: 4.5 LTS to 5.3 (for Moodle 4.1 to 4.4 use version 1.7.0)
  • PHP: 8.2 or later
  • Browser: modern browser with WebGL (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari); VR and AR need a WebXR-capable browser and device
  • AI assistant (optional): an AI provider with text generation configured in Moodle's AI settings

Privacy

Models, hotspots, grades and analytics stay on your site and are covered by the Moodle Privacy API. Only two optional features contact outside services: the AI assistant sends the text a teacher types to the AI provider the administrator set up in Moodle's AI settings, and collaborative mode connects participants through a PeerJS broker (the public PeerJS cloud or your own server) while chat and audio travel directly between their browsers.

How it works

  1. Upload: add your .glb or .gltf models (optionally a .usdz for iPhone AR).
  2. Annotate: Shift + click on the model to place hotspots and assessment points.
  3. Engage: students explore, collaborate and complete the integrated activities.
