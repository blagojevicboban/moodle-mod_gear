GEAR: Immersive 3D, AR and VR Learning

Upload a 3D model, place interactive hotspots on it and let students explore, answer quizzes and learn together, right inside Moodle.

What's New in v1.8.0

  • Accessible dialogs: the browser pop-ups for deleting a hotspot and for the AI topic are replaced by Moodle dialogs with focus handling.
  • Text alternative for the 3D scene: a new Scene description setting, a labelled canvas and a hidden hotspot list for screen reader and keyboard users.
  • Chat: the sender shown for a message comes from the server's session list, not from the message; verified end to end between two browsers.
  • Translations: remaining English texts in the viewer are language strings now.
  • Included since 1.7.1: server-side quiz grading and unlocking, AI through Moodle's AI subsystem, completion rules, group mode, Draco/Meshopt models, several models per scene, AR Quick Look for iPhone and iPad.

Overview

GEAR (Geospatial Educational Augmented Reality) is a Moodle activity module that bridges the gap between traditional e-learning and immersive 3D technology. Educators upload 3D models, annotate them with hotspots and assessment points, and students explore, collaborate and are graded inside Moodle.

Core features

3D viewer and interaction

glTF / GLB models (also Draco and Meshopt compressed, several per scene) with orbit, pan and zoom, adjustable lighting and background.

Interactive hotspots

Information, quiz, audio, video (MP4, YouTube, Vimeo) and teleport points placed directly on the model, with visual X/Y/Z gizmos for precise placement.

Spatial quizzes and completion

Multiple-choice questions in the scene, checked on the server and synced with the Moodle gradebook. Completion rules: open every hotspot and/or earn a minimum of quiz points.

Escape-room style unlocking

Branching scenarios: a hotspot stays hidden until the student solves the required quiz. Teleport waypoints guide the camera.

Optional AI assistant and text-to-speech

Generate hotspot texts and quiz questions with the AI providers of Moodle (off by default). Information hotspots can be read aloud.

Optional collaborative mode

Shared sessions with 3D avatars and peer-to-peer voice and text chat, group aware. Off by default for new installs.

Analytics, leaderboard and spatial audio

Teacher dashboard with hotspot clicks and AR/VR starts, a leaderboard, and positional audio that reacts to the student's distance.

Accessible and fast

Keyboard navigation, screen reader announcements, labelled dialogs and reduced motion support; optimised WebGL scaling for mobile devices and VR headsets.

AR/VR and mobile

  • WebXR ready: one-click VR, and AR where the device supports it, without installing an app.
  • iPhone and iPad: the 3D viewer works; for AR upload a .usdz file with the same name as the model and it opens in AR Quick Look.
  • Moodle Mobile app: supported.

Requirements

  • Moodle: 4.5 LTS to 5.3 (for Moodle 4.1 to 4.4 use version 1.7.0)
  • PHP: 8.2 or later
  • Browser: modern browser with WebGL (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari); VR and AR need a WebXR-capable browser and device
  • AI assistant (optional): an AI provider with text generation configured in Moodle's AI settings

Privacy

Models, hotspots, grades and analytics stay on your site and are covered by the Moodle Privacy API. Only two optional features contact outside services: the AI assistant sends the text a teacher types to the AI provider the administrator set up in Moodle's AI settings, and collaborative mode connects participants through a PeerJS broker (the public PeerJS cloud or your own server) while chat and audio travel directly between their browsers.

How it works

  1. Upload: add your .glb or .gltf models (optionally a .usdz for iPhone AR).
  2. Annotate: Shift + click on the model to place hotspots and assessment points.
  3. Engage: students explore, collaborate and complete the integrated activities.
