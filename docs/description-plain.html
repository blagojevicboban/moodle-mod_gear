GEAR: Immersive 3D, AR and VR Learning

Upload a 3D model, place interactive hotspots on it and let students explore, answer quizzes and learn together, right inside Moodle.

What's New in v4.0.0

  • 3D Pin-Drop, Assembly and Sequence Quizzes: students click a 3D surface point graded by tolerance radius, click parts in exact order, or reassemble an exploded 3D model step by step, plus measure 3-point vertex angles in degrees.
  • 3D Analytics Heatmap: managers toggle color-coded 3D halos and error-rate summaries directly on the model.
  • Student Study / Flashcard Mode: blur hotspot labels so students can self-test and track revealed parts without affecting Gradebook scores.
  • Tour View Capture and 3D Surface Ink: capture camera and explode views in one click, auto-generate tours from hotspots, and draw live 3D ink strokes over PeerJS.
  • 3D Model Library, Split View and Study Sheet Export: start with 5 built-in multi-part models, compare solid vs. wireframe with Before/After Split View, inspect mesh/triangle stats, and export printable PDF/HTML study sheets.

Overview

GEAR (Geospatial Educational Augmented Reality) bridges traditional e-learning and immersive 3D technology. Educators upload 3D models, annotate them with hotspots and assessment points, and students explore, collaborate and are graded inside Moodle.

Features

  • 3D viewer and inspection: glTF and GLB models (including Draco, Meshopt and KTX2 compression, several per scene) with orbit, pan and zoom, exploded view, X-ray, cross-section clipping, 2-point distance and 3-point angle measurement, studio PMREM lighting with exposure control, glTF animations and material variants.
  • Interactive hotspots: information, quiz, audio, video (MP4, YouTube, Vimeo) and teleport points placed on the model with visual X/Y/Z gizmos.
  • Spatial quizzes and completion: single-choice, multi-choice, free-text with wildcards, 3D mesh click, pin-drop, sequence, assembly and question-bank rotation, all graded server-side and synced with the Moodle gradebook.
  • Guided tours and branching: step-by-step camera tours with AI narration, plus escape-room style unlocking when prerequisite quizzes are solved.
  • Optional AI assistant and text-to-speech: generate hotspot texts, quizzes, auto-annotations and tour narration with Moodle core AI (off by default), plus read-aloud for info hotspots.
  • Optional collaborative mode: shared sessions with 3D avatars, live laser pointer, follow-presenter camera, 3D surface ink and peer-to-peer WebRTC voice and text chat, group aware (off by default).
  • Progress, analytics and accessibility: detailed student reports (CSV/Excel, Report Builder), leaderboard, keyboard navigation, screen reader scene descriptions, reduced motion support and lower render resolution for mobile/VR.

AR/VR and mobile

  • WebXR ready: one-click VR, and AR where supported, without installing an app.
  • iPhone and iPad: the 3D viewer works; for AR upload a .usdz file with the same name as the model to open in AR Quick Look.
  • Moodle app: displays the description, student progress and in-app view.

Requirements

  • Moodle: 4.5 LTS to 5.3 (for Moodle 4.1 to 4.4 use version 1.7.0)
  • PHP: 8.2 or later
  • Browser: modern browser with WebGL (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari); VR/AR need a WebXR-capable browser and device
  • AI assistant (optional): an AI provider with text generation configured in Moodle's AI settings

Privacy

Models, hotspots, grades and analytics stay on your site and are covered by the Moodle Privacy API. Only two optional features contact outside services: the AI assistant sends the text a teacher types to the configured Moodle AI provider, and collaborative mode connects participants through a PeerJS broker while chat and audio travel directly between browsers.
